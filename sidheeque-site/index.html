About

A school leader who builds strong teachers, teams and institutions

More than ten years dedicated to building schools where both students and teachers thrive — in the classroom, in assessment, and in the training room.

I'm Aboobacker Sidheeque — an educator and transformational academic leader, with more than ten years dedicated to building schools where both students and teachers thrive.

My work sits at the meeting point of leadership, assessment and teacher development. I currently serve as the Lead Educator at Pearl Wisdom School, Dubai, and Academic Advisor to MES Central School Vengara and Meridian Public School Pandallur. Earlier, I led Al Haramain English School, Kozhikode, and The Oxford School, Thiruvananthapuram; Peace International School, Trikaripur; and Eranad Knowledge City Public School, Manjeri, as their academic leader. Each role has deepened one conviction: good schools are built by empowered, well-supported teachers.

I believe assessment should reveal learning, not merely rank it. Much of my work has gone into competency-based assessment — designing tools and frameworks that help teachers understand how children actually learn. My most significant contribution is a comprehensive 360° Holistic Progress Card for CBSE schools: built for the foundational stage and balancing competency development with learning outcomes, it has since been introduced in multiple schools across Kerala. A PG Diploma in Educational Assessment and an MA in Education from Azim Premji University anchor this work in both research and practice.

As an empanelled Resource Person with the CBSE Centre of Excellence, Thiruvananthapuram — and now with Oxford University Press — I've led over 100 training sessions for educators nationwide, on Strengthening Assessment & Evaluation Practices, Learning Outcomes and Pedagogy, NEP 2020 and NCF, Life Skills, Happy Classrooms, mental health and wellbeing, and engaging parents as partners in education.

Leading schools has also made me a people-and-administration leader. Across these institutions I've handled recruitment and onboarding, built staff-development and mentoring systems, designed 360° performance-appraisal frameworks, and shaped the policies, workflows and culture that let teams do their best work — an approach anchored by an MBA in Human Resource Management. Much of this is now built into Inspire OS, the school platform I architected to bring teacher appraisal, academic analytics and administrative workflows into one place.

Whether I'm leading a school, designing an assessment framework, or standing before a room of teachers, the goal is the same — to inspire, empower and transform the people who shape young lives.

My educational philosophy
Education is not the filling of a vessel, but the kindling of a flame. Our role as educators is to ignite curiosity, nurture potential, and empower every learner to discover their unique path to excellence.

I believe in creating learning environments where students are active participants in their own growth, where teachers are lifelong learners, and where schools are communities of continuous improvement and innovation.

My vision

Beyond the classroom

True education transcends textbooks

It is about shaping character, fostering curiosity, and building resilient communities.

I believe that every child possesses an innate potential waiting to be unlocked. As educators, our role is not merely to instruct, but to inspire — to create environments where students feel safe to explore, question and grow.
