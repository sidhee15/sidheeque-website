Who I am

I'm an educator and school leader with over a decade of experience shaping academic excellence in CBSE schools. As a Resource Person for both the CBSE Centre of Excellence, Thiruvananthapuram and Oxford University Press, I work with teachers across the country to turn national frameworks — NEP 2020, NCF, and competency-based assessment — into everyday classroom practice.
